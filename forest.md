# The Forest Path

You decide to follow the narrow trail deeper into the forest.

The trees grow taller with every step, blocking most of the sunlight. Strange noises echo through the woods, and the air feels cool and damp. After walking for nearly an hour, you discover a clearing with a glowing blue pond in the center.

As you approach the pond, you notice something unusual. On one side of the clearing stands an ancient stone statue covered in vines. On the other side, a small wooden bridge crosses a stream and disappears into the dense forest.
